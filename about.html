PRODUCT MANAGER & MAKER

Hello, I'm Ikzaaz.

I'm a Computer Science graduate from Universitas Brawijaya, currently based in Batam, Indonesia, and figuring out my way into the world of Product Management.

I've always been curious about how things work, but even more curious about why people need them in the first place. That curiosity has taken me through technology, design, product operations, content creation, and quite a few leadership roles along the way.

I like connecting the dots between people, problems, and possibilities. Whether it's building digital products, bringing ideas to life, or getting people together to make things happen, I'm happiest when there's something meaningful to figure out.

Outside of work, you'll probably find me exploring new technology, experimenting with AI, or going down another internet rabbit hole. This website is a pretty good example of all three.

Background

I started out studying Computer Science at Universitas Brawijaya, thinking my future would revolve around writing code. Turns out, I was just as interested in the people using the technology as the technology itself.

University became my playground for trying different things. I led student initiatives, managed events, worked in social media, taught mobile app development, and somehow found myself holding a microphone at international competitions.

Professionally, I've explored content creation at Digimarly and product operations at dibimbing.id, where I helped manage learning programs, coordinate teams, and improve experiences based on user feedback.

Along the way, I discovered that understanding the problem, aligning people, and figuring out what to build excited me more than jumping straight into development.

That's what eventually drew me toward Product Management. Not exactly a straight line, but looking back, the dots connect.

Interests

I've never been particularly good at sticking to just one thing. My interests tend to overlap, evolve, and occasionally turn into side projects.

Right now, I'm particularly into:

Product Management UI/UX Design AI & Emerging Tech Vibe Coding Digital Products Content & Storytelling

I enjoy exploring how technology changes the way we work, create, and interact. Lately, that's meant experimenting with AI tools like GPT-6 and Gemini Argon to turn ideas into something tangible.

I'm also a big believer that good products start with good conversations. So naturally, I enjoy meeting new people, exchanging perspectives, and learning from experiences outside my usual bubble.

And when curiosity gets the better of me? Well, sometimes it turns into an entire website.

Current Role

I'm currently a Junior Developer (Learner) at Apple Developer Academy @ Infinite Learning in Batam, where I work with multidisciplinary teams to explore problems, challenge assumptions, and build user-centered digital product prototypes.

Through Apple's Challenge-Based Learning approach, I've been involved in the product development process, from discovering user needs and conducting research to shaping solutions, prototyping, and validating ideas.

Before joining the Academy, I worked in B2C Product Operations at dibimbing.id, helping manage four bootcamp programs serving over 170 students while coordinating with Class Managers, mentors, and cross-functional teams.

That experience taught me something valuable: building a product is one thing, but making sure it actually works for the people using it is another.

These days, I'm particularly interested in opportunities where I can bring together product thinking, user experience, technology, and business strategy.
